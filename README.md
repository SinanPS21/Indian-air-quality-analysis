# India Air Quality Analysis (2010–2023)

A **data analytics deep dive** into India’s air quality crisis, built with **Tableau** and powered by the **India Air Quality Master Dataset** — covering **595,882 observations** across **241 cities** and **31 states and territories** over **14 years**.

---

## 📊 Overview

This project visualizes and analyzes India’s air pollution trends from **2010 to 2023**, focusing on **PM2.5**, **NO₂**, and other key pollutants.  
It highlights regional disparities, seasonal patterns, and the impact of major events like the **2020 COVID lockdown**.

---

## 🧩 Dataset Summary

| Metric | Value | Description |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| Total Observations | 595,882 | Daily PM2.5 readings across all stations |
| Cities | 241 | Urban and semi‑urban locations |
| States | 31 | Including union territories |
| Years | 14 | Continuous data from 2010–2023 |
| Pollutants | PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, CO, SO₂, O₃, NH₃, Benzene, Toluene | Comprehensive pollutant tracking |

---

## 🌍 Key Insights

- **India’s air is 4.5× worse than WHO limits** (68 µg/m³ vs 15 µg/m³).  
- **70.7 % of days are unsafe to breathe** across all cities.  
- **Byrnihat (Meghalaya)** is the most polluted city — *229.2 µg/m³*.  
- **Delhi** ranks as the most polluted state — *110.1 µg/m³*.  
- **Bihar** dominates the top 10 polluted cities list.  
- **Winter** is the deadliest season — *180 + µg/m³* average PM2.5.  
- **COVID lockdown (2020)** caused a historic **16 % drop** in pollution.

## 🧠 Key Takeaways

1. **The Crisis Is Real:** India’s air quality exceeds WHO limits by 4.5×.  
2. **Change Is Possible:** The 2020 lockdown proved human activity drives pollution.  
3. **North India Needs Urgent Action:** Delhi, Bihar, and UP require targeted interventions.

---

## 🛠️ Recommendations

- **Stricter Emission Standards** — enforce tighter vehicle norms.  
- **Crop Burning Ban** — year‑round ban with farmer compensation.  
- **Expand AQI Monitoring** — real‑time coverage for Tier 2 & 3 cities.
